Engineering foundation

The simplicity is intentional. The engineering is not simple.

The public site should stay clean on the homepage, while technical diligence can expand into a deeper technology page or data room.

Actuation

Bilateral linear actuation and motorized angular control create the mechanical basis for controlled traction.

Sensing

Load-cell force sensing and inertial measurement convert device state into actionable feedback.

Control

Closed-loop logic supports smoother adjustment and reduces uncontrolled overshoot in the prototype architecture.
